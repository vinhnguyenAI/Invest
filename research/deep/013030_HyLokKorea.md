## Business in two sentences
Hy-Lok makes precision stainless-steel instrumentation tube fittings, valves and manifolds. Its customers are shipyards (LNG carriers and offshore units), EPC contractors and plant owners in oil & gas, petrochemicals, power and semiconductor fabs, who pay a premium for leak-proof, certified parts whose cost is trivial next to the cost of a failure. Most sales are exports or indirect exports (via shipyards and EPCs).

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩224.6bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **21%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩47.2bn → **normalised EY 12.2%** (P/E 8.2x) vs hurdle 8.5%. Ex-cash, the EY would be higher still (net cash is not verified).
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. OPM has run at 22–29% since 2022, and NPM at 24–26% from FY24 to TTM. Large interest income on net cash pushes NPM close to OPM. 21% sits below every recent year and roughly matches the weaker FY22 operating level (22% OPM) less tax.
  2. Drivers. Pricing is niche and oligopolistic (Swagelok/Parker are the global peers, with similar high margins). Mix is moving toward higher-value LNG-carrier and offshore fittings. Input is stainless bar (nickel), whose costs are passed through with a lag. A weak KRW helps.
- Bear case: **16%** (shipbuilding and plant-capex downcycle, a nickel spike, KRW strength; similar to a pre-2021 trough) → ₩35.9bn → **EY 9.3%**.

## Owner & governance

There has been friction with minority shareholders over the 상근감사 appointment (Asiae, Mar-2023), and an article refers to a "경영권 분쟁". Check the latest status on DART. ## Balance sheet
Strong net cash with negligible debt (Daishin estimate tables; the ~8.5% 부채비율 is cited by alphadistill on an unverified basis). No dilution history was found. *Estimate, not verified against the FY2025 BS.*

## Growth runway
Revenue stagnated from 2022 to 2024, in part because Russian-gas-sanction-driven orders boosted profit rather than volume. FY25 (+12.5%) and 1H26 (+9.8%) show re-acceleration on the LNG-carrier and offshore supercycle and on semis and US/Middle-East plant capex. Korea Investment & Securities expects quarterly revenue to rise to ₩63bn by 4Q26. If FY26 lands at ~₩240bn and FY27 keeps growing ~10%, the stock may meet the growth rule in 1–2 years. Today it does not.

## Key risks
Shipbuilding and plant-capex cyclicality. Nickel and stainless input costs. The KRW/USD rate. Competition from Swagelok and Parker. Succession and minority-shareholder friction. Revenue has stayed range-bound for long periods in the past.
