## Business in two sentences
Silicon2 is a B2B export platform for Korean cosmetics. It buys from hundreds of indie K-beauty brands (COSRX, Beauty of Joseon, Anua, etc.) and sells and ships them to retailers and distributors in 100+ countries from its own logistics hubs in Europe, the US and elsewhere. Brands pay with distributor margin (~30% gross) for overseas reach, and buyers pay for one-stop sourcing, fast delivery and credit.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩1,358bn (TTM). FY26E is ~₩1.6tn+ given 1H26 ₩749bn and the run-rate.
- Normalised net margin: **13.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩183bn → **normalised EY 8.8%** (P/E 11.4x) vs hurdle 8.5%. On FY26E revenue of ₩1.6tn it would be ~10.3%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History and mechanism. GPM is ~30% (company guidance). OPM was ~18% in FY25, 18.6% in 1Q26 and 20.6% in 2Q26, against company guidance of 18%. After ~22% tax plus small interest and FX, NPM is ~14–15%, so 13.5% is a haircut to the run-rate.
  2. Scale. Fixed costs (warehouses in Poland/US, IT) are leveraged as volume grows. The 4Q24 "shock" (OPM dip from FX/logistics/one-off costs) shows a quarter can drop to ~13–14% OPM, which is why I don't use the 2Q26 peak.
- Bear case: 10% NPM (brands go direct with Amazon/own distributors, price competition, freight/FX swings, Middle East and Russia disruption) → ₩136bn → **EY 6.5%**.

## Owner & governance
Founder 김성운 (CEO since 2002) is the largest shareholder and runs the company. Insider and related-party selling at highs in 2024 (spouse, ~1.54% by two related parties, ₩31bn) and in 1H25 for gift tax is a mild negative. Glenwood Credit holds RCPS convertible from 2026-03-23, which is an overhang.

## Balance sheet
Not verified. The company had borrowings from building overseas logistics centres. ₩50bn of the 2025 RCPS proceeds plus 1Q25 cash were used to repay debt. Working capital is heavy (inventory and receivables, e.g. ~₩30bn of Middle East stock). Given cumulative NI of ~₩170bn+ in FY25 and ~₩140bn+ in 1H26, I estimate net cash, but the investor must check the FY25 and 1H26 balance sheets on DART.

## Growth runway
Europe (₩161.8bn in 1Q26, a record), North America (+78% in 2Q26) and CIS are growing fast, while Asia and the Middle East are softer. Global K-beauty demand plus new-brand onboarding gives a multi-year runway. Hana raised its target price to ₩59,000 in Aug-2026.

## Key risks
Brand disintermediation, where big brands (COSRX/Amorepacific etc.) shift to direct or other distributors. Concentration in a handful of hit brands. Working-capital and FX risk. Geopolitics (Russia, Middle East). Tariff risk in the US. RCPS dilution and insider selling.
