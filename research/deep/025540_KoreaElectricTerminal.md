## Business in two sentences
KET makes electrical connectors, terminals, junction boxes and EV high-voltage connectors that wire up cars, and sells them mainly to Hyundai/Kia and their tier-1 harness makers. Customers pay because KET is a qualified, long-standing supplier and switching connector platforms mid-model is costly.
