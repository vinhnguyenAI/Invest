## Business in two sentences
(Estimate) KET makes electrical connectors and terminals that join wiring harnesses and modules in cars and electronic devices. It sells them mainly to Hyundai/Kia and their harness suppliers, plus global OEMs; customers pay for qualified, spec-in parts that are costly to switch once designed in.
