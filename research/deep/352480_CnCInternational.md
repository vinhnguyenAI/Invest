## Business in two sentences
C&C International is a Korean ODM that develops and manufactures colour cosmetics, especially lip products, for indie K-beauty brands and global prestige brands. Brands pay it for formulation, fast new-product development and contract manufacturing so they don't need their own factories.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩292.8bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: 8% (generous estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩23.4bn → **normalised EY 8.6%** (P/E 11.6x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin:** FY22–23 NPM of 13–15% came during the indie K-beauty lip-tint boom with high utilisation. FY24–25 saw a key domestic customer's orders drop sharply, and 2026 has brought rising costs and losses (new plant/US expansion). ODM peers (Cosmax, Kolmar) earn ~5–8% NPM. 8% assumes utilisation recovers, but current trading (~4% OPM) is far below that.
- Bear case: 4% NPM → ₩11.7bn → **EY 4.3%**.

## Owner & governance
The founding 배 family controls ~66% (배은철 외 3인), and a family member is CEO. Owner-run passes, but the high stake leaves a small float.

## Balance sheet
Historically net cash (cash ₩68–88bn in FY23/FY24 per brokers). A report mention of a large rights-issue plan is unverified and likely a parsing error. Check DART.

## Growth runway
Overseas orders (US/Europe brands) are growing, but domestic indie-brand orders have shrunk. Revenue has been flat since FY24 at ~₩285–290bn. That is not a growth phase.

## Key risks
Customer concentration. Fragmented, low-barrier ODM competition. Margin collapse in 2026. Losses at new overseas operations. Thin float.
