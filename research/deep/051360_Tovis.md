## Business in two sentences
Tovis (founded 1998) designs and assembles specialised LCD monitors (curved, touch, high-brightness) for slot machines and other gaming and industrial uses. It sells them to the global casino-equipment OEMs (mostly US), which pay for reliability, custom form factors and quick turnaround. Its fast-growing automotive display-module business (an LG Display partner) was spun off into 네오뷰 (split date 1-Jul-2026, relisted ~20-Jul-2026).

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: **₩280bn (FY2025 estimate for the remaining casino/industrial business; not verified)**
- Normalised net margin: **7.0%**
- Normalised net income: ₩19.6bn → **normalised EY 18.9%** (P/E 5.3x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. Pre-split group OPM was 9–11% in 2024–25, and casino monitors were historically the higher-margin segment; auto modules are lower margin and capex-heavy. A 9–10% OPM on casino alone, less tax and some standalone HQ cost, gives about 7% net.
  2. Inputs are LCD panels, which are a commodity in oversupply. Sales are in USD, so a weak KRW helps.
- Bear case 4.5% net (tariff-driven price cuts from US OEMs, standalone overhead, panel cost spike) → ₩12.6bn → **EY 12.2%**.
