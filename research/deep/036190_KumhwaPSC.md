## Business in two sentences
Kumhwa PSC is an independent (non-KEPCO) maintenance contractor. Korean power generators, mostly KEPCO's five thermal gencos plus private IPPs, pay it under multi-year contracts to keep coal and LNG plants running through routine maintenance (경상정비) and periodic planned overhauls (계획예방정비). Since 2023 it also operates and maintains water-treatment and desalination plants through its 100% subsidiary 금화정수, bought for ₩85bn.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩447.8bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **6.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩29.1bn → **normalised EY 15.1%** (P/E 6.6x) vs hurdle 8.5%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. OPM has run at 10–13% from FY22 to FY25 (8.4% TTM after a weak 1Q26), and NPM was 7.2% in FY25 and 6.7% TTM. A 6.5% NPM is roughly an 8.5–9% OPM after 22% tax, plus interest income on cash. That is below every full year since FY22 and about equal to TTM.
  2. Drivers. Revenue is labour-based service on multi-year genco contracts with low capital intensity. Swings come from the 1.5–2-year overhaul cycle (lumpy year-on-year) and one-offs (FY23). The water O&M subsidiary has a stable contract base. Input cost is mainly wages, which are passed through in contract re-pricing with a lag.
- Bear case: **4.5%**. Genco budget cuts, coal-unit closures and wage inflation not passed through would push OPM to ~6% → ₩20.2bn → **EY 10.4%**, still above the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Chairman 김성기 (long-time controlling shareholder; founder per memory, *verify*) is a registered co-CEO. The family and related parties hold 37.5%. In the 2010s a PE/activist investor (T-Turn Capital) built a stake (etoday 2014; *current status unknown*). Historically the company kept a large cash hoard and paid out ~25% of earnings (eBest); the dividend yield is ~4.7% (butler.works).

## Balance sheet
Historically very cash-rich: net cash was ₩138bn in 2020, about equal to its market cap then, and ₩126bn at end-2021, with financial debt of only ~₩4bn. After the ₩85bn Ecobit Engineering purchase (Dec-2022) and three years of ~₩25–30bn profit, my estimate of FY2025 net cash is ~₩80–110bn and 부채비율 ~25–35%. **Not verified.** Pull the FY2025 BS from DART before any purchase.

## Growth runway
Positives: re-acceleration in FY25 and 1H26. New LNG combined-cycle plants are replacing coal and still need O&M. Water/desalination O&M is growing, nuclear and renewables O&M could be adjacent opportunities, and margin pressure has been limited. Negatives: Korea's 11th Basic Plan retires a large share of coal units by the late 2030s, which shrinks the core 경상정비 pool. FY24 was flat (+0.8%), which shows the overhaul cycle can stall growth for a year.

## Key risks
Coal phase-out. Dependence on the KEPCO gencos, which control budgets and tenders. Lumpy overhaul cycles. Wage inflation and safety regulation (Serious Accidents Punishment Act). Acquisition integration. The auditor and the FY2025 balance sheet are unverified.
