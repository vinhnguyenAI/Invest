## Business in two sentences
THN makes automotive wiring harnesses (the vehicle "nervous system") and electronic control modules, mainly in overseas plants, and supplies them to Hyundai-Kia and Hyundai Mobis. The OEM pays per vehicle on model-cycle contracts; higher-spec SUVs/hybrids (e.g. new Palisade) carry more harness content per car.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩989bn (FY2025; TTM likely slightly higher given +13.9% Q2 growth)
- Normalised net margin: **3.5%**
- Normalised net income: ₩34.6bn → **normalised EY ~42%** (P/E ~2.4x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal"**:
  1. History: net margin −1.3% (2022, copper/logistics squeeze), 4.7% (2023), 5.1% (2024), 6.9% (2025), 5.3% (1H26). Four-year average ≈ 3.9%; 3.5% is below the average and below every year except 2022.
  2. Drivers of the 2025 peak (Palisade/hybrid volume, JSN business integration scale, purchasing leverage) are partly structural, but Q2'26 OP fell 25% YoY on +14% revenue, showing peak margins are fading; harness peers (Kyungshin, Yura) earn ~2–4% net. Net income ≈ OP in 2024–25 implies low tax/financial cost which may normalise, so haircut to 3.5%.
- What would make the margin lower (bear case): copper price spike, wage inflation at overseas plants, Hyundai price-downs, KRW appreciation, tax normalisation. Bear 2.0% → ₩19.8bn → **EY ~24%**.

## Balance sheet
- Latest quarter (Investing.com): total liabilities ~₩342bn, total equity ~₩248bn → 부채비율 ~138% (derived) — *partly verified*; "debt/equity 42.5%" suggests interest-bearing debt ~₩105bn. Cash not found → net debt unverified (estimate ₩50–80bn net debt, i.e. <1x EBITDA of ~₩90bn).
- Liabilities are inflated by trade payables typical of JIT suppliers; with EBITDA ~₩90bn, net debt/EBITDA is likely <1.5x, so the 부채비율 breach is a soft fail.
- No CB/BW/rights issues found.

## Growth runway
Content-per-vehicle growth (SUV, hybrid, EV high-voltage harness) and rising share within Hyundai Group (12.8% in 2023 → 13.3% in 2024, per broker report); new Palisade cycle; possible robot/electronics harness diversification (speculative). Post-acquisition growth will slow to low-teens/single digits.

## Key risks
- Single-customer group (Hyundai-Kia/Mobis) concentration and price-downs.
- Margin mean-reversion already visible in Q2'26.
- Related-party dealings with 제이에스엔; low payout; thin liquidity / small cap.
- Auditor unknown.

Net debt is **unverified** (estimate ₩50–80bn, <1x EBITDA, which passes the 1.5x test).
- **JSN acquisition:** Asia Economy/Hana (2026-03-18) describes the ₩9.8bn purchase of the wiring-harness business as from **관계사 제이에스엔** (an affiliate). Two further searches found no DART-level detail on the exact relationship (e.g. whether 이광연 or family own JSN). Treat it as a **related-party transaction**. It is small (~14% of FY25 NI) but it contributes to FY25's revenue jump (+52%), so the organic 3y CAGR is lower than 28%.
