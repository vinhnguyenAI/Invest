## Business in two sentences
Kumyang Green Power does electrical and plant construction, and routine maintenance, for power stations (coal/LNG/renewables) and industrial plants. Its customers are utilities and EPC primes that pay per contract. It is now trying to become a renewable developer and owner through affiliate SPCs such as the 해오름에너지 project.

## Owner & governance
The founding family controls ~34%, and succession to the son is under way (Sep-2025 gift). A 2024 Daum article ("재주는 금양이, 떡은 왜 금양그린파워가") discusses confusion and trading tied to the unrelated-by-name 금양 (battery) stock. There are related-party guarantees to the renewable SPC.

## Balance sheet
Moderate leverage (~72% 부채비율), but contingent liabilities from the ₩60.6bn of guarantees are large relative to the ₩86bn market cap and ~₩94bn of equity.

## Growth runway
Demand for grid and renewable construction exists, but revenue growth is modest (+8.9% FY25, +3% FY26E) and profitability has not followed.

## Key risks
Contract losses. PF guarantee calls on renewable SPCs. Customer concentration. Name-confusion volatility.
