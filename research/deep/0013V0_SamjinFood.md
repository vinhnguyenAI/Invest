## Business in two sentences
Samjin makes premium fish cakes (eomuk) and fish-cake snacks from imported surimi in Busan. It sells them to Korean consumers through its own shops, supermarkets and online, to B2B/food-service and packaged-snack customers, and increasingly as exports (China, US, Japan). Customers pay for a heritage "Busan eomuk" brand at a premium to commodity fish cakes.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩113.4bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **4.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩5.1bn → **normalised EY 9.6%** (P/E 10.4x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History: OPM has climbed from ~2% (FY22, OP ₩1.8bn) to 5.0% (FY24) to 5.5% (FY25) to 5.9% (TTM) on scale, the B2B packaged-snack mix (₩14.2bn in 1H26) and exports (+90% in 1H26). FY25 NPM was 4.7%, the first clean year after the RCPS valuation losses. 4.5% assumes the FY25/TTM level holds but does not expand further.
  2. Peers: Korean processed-food peers (사조대림, CJ-type seafood) earn ~2–5% NPM. Samjin's premium brand and its own-store channel justify the upper end, but 2Q26 showed how sensitive it is to surimi prices (+7% QoQ purchase cost, a −₩0.5bn quarterly OP) and to delays in passing B2B prices through.
- Bear case: 3.0% NPM (surimi cost spike persists, the plant ramp adds depreciation) → ₩3.4bn → **EY 6.4%**.

## Owner & governance
3rd-generation owner-CEO 박용준 holds 41.94% after the IPO, and family (박성우 이사) takes the group above 50%. The company listed on 22-Dec-2025 at ₩7,600, the top of the band, with record retail demand (3,224:1). The share price has since fallen ~30%. The pre-IPO investors' RCPS were converted, and post-lockup overhang is a risk.

## Balance sheet
❓ Not verified. The IPO raised ₩15.2bn (gross). Food processing with its own stores is moderately capital-intensive, and a new 장림 plant investment is underway. Check net debt and 부채비율 in the 1H26 반기보고서. Pre-IPO RCPS created valuation losses (FY23/FY24); those are now converted.

## Growth runway
Exports (China snack channel, the US) and B2B packaged snacks are growing quickly, and domestic retail is mature. Revenue grew from ~₩1.8bn (2015) to over ₩100bn. Low-teens growth looks plausible, but 1H26 +7.7% is below the 10% rule.

## Key risks
Surimi (Alaska pollock / tropical fish) price and FX (USD imports). Seasonality (2Q/3Q off-season losses). Concentration in a mature domestic category. IPO-related overhang. Thin margins leave little room for error.
