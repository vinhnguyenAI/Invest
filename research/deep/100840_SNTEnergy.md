## Business in two sentences
SNT Energy designs and fabricates large heat-transfer equipment: air coolers for LNG liquefaction and refinery projects (for EPCs such as Bechtel and JGC/Fluor), and heat-recovery steam generators (HRSG) and condensers for gas and nuclear power plants. EPC contractors and utilities buy on a project basis, and revenue follows the global LNG and power-plant capex cycle.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ~₩568bn (TTM, estimate)
- Normalised net margin: 9% (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩51bn → **normalised EY 8.7%** (P/E 11.6x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin:** OPM was 6–8% in FY23–24 and then 18–20% in FY25–1H26, when SNT Energy took selective high-margin LNG air-cooler orders during the US LNG FID wave. Over a full cycle a ~12% OPM / ~9% NPM is plausible, given its global #1 position in air coolers and new HRSG demand (Korean LNG conversions, e.g. Hadong ₩128bn). The 2023–24 record shows margins of half that in normal years.
- Bear case: 5% NPM (a return to the FY23–24 margin on lower volume) → ₩28bn → **EY 4.8%**.
