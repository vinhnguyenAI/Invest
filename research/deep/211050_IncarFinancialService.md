## Business in two sentences
Incar is Korea's largest listed independent insurance agency. Its ~24,700 contracted agents sell life and non-life policies from many insurers to retail customers. Insurers pay Incar sales commissions (mostly front-loaded in years 1–2), and Incar passes most of them on to its agents.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩1,192.9bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **6.0%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩71.6bn → **normalised EY 15.2%** (P/E 6.6x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. NPM was ~5.2% in FY22, 7.4% in FY24, 6.9% in FY25 and 7.0% in 1H26. The GA model passes ~85–90% of commissions to agents, so a 9–10% OPM is a scale-driven ceiling. 6.0% sits below the recent run-rate.
  2. Regulation drags margins. The 1200% rule applies to GA agents from Jul 2026, a 4-year commission instalment (분급) starts in 2027, and 7 years from 2029. These raise recruiting and retention costs and delay cash. 2Q26 already carried ~₩6bn of one-offs (FSS fine, a golf sponsorship). Insurers' post-IFRS17 push for protection products is a tailwind that could fade.
- Bear case: 4.0% (regulatory compression plus a slowdown in new contracts) → ₩47.7bn → **EY 10.1%**

## Owner & governance
Founder 최병채 (registered director since 2010, chairman/CEO) holds 23.10%. Affiliate 콜로세움미디어 (62.5% owned by 최인렬, a family member, with the founder's spouse formerly a director) has been buying shares, funded by loans secured on the founder's pledged shares. Press reads this as tax-efficient succession. Executives receive RSAs (treasury-share bonuses).

## Balance sheet
FY2024 부채비율 ~380% (560.2/147.3). In 2023 Incar borrowed from Meritz to fund agent recruitment and settlement payments. Net cash/debt for FY2025 was not verified. PBR is ~2.2x with ROE ~32% (stockhub). No CB/rights issues were found in the searches.

## Growth runway
Agent headcount rose from 20,651 to 24,724 in 1H26 (+19.7%) and revenue grew +36.5% YoY. Industry consolidation toward large GAs continues, but the 1200% rule and the 분급 regime may slow recruitment from 2027.

## Key risks
Commission regulation (1200% rule, 분급, disclosure of sales commissions). Chargebacks if policies lapse. Mis-selling fines (an FSS inspection already led to a penalty). Agent poaching wars. Founder share pledge and succession transactions. Leverage.
