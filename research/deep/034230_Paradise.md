## Business in two sentences
Paradise runs four of Korea's foreign-only casinos and the Paradise City integrated resort, 55% owned through a JV with Sega Sammy. It earns gaming win from Japanese, Chinese and other foreign players plus hotel, F&B and leisure revenue at the resorts.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩1,194.3bn (TTM, estimate)
- Normalised net margin (attributable to owners): **7.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩89.6bn → **normalised EY 10.1%** (P/E 9.9x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. Attributable NPM was 6.3% in FY23, 7.1% in FY24 and ~8% in FY25E. The total-NI margin of 12.5% in FY25 overstates it because ~30–35% of NI belongs to Sega Sammy's 45% of Paradise City. Before COVID (2017–19), heavy Paradise City depreciation and interest kept NPM in the low single digits, so 7.5% is a mid-cycle level with a mature IR.
  2. Drivers. Visa-free entry for Chinese groups and Japanese demand supported drop in 2025–26. Against that, OPM is falling in 2026 (labour, marketing and VIP-acquisition costs, plus ~₩5bn/quarter of Hyatt Regency losses), and 1H26 OPM was 11.9% vs 15.1% in 1H25. Hold rate is a source of noise. Interest on ~₩380bn+ of net debt and the NCI leakage limit how much of OP converts to NI.
- Bear case: 5.0% (lower hold, China VIP slowdown, Hyatt integration losses) → ₩59.7bn → **EY 6.7%**, below the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Family control is solid (45.26% via Paradise Global, which is 67.3% owned by 전필립, with his three children at 6.7% each). But 전필립 has no board seat since 2024 and the company runs on a professional co-CEO structure (최종환/임준신, Mar 2026). The 2026 AGM introduced cumulative voting and "independent director" terminology, which is positive for minority holders. Overall this looks closer to "family-controlled, professionally managed" than to owner-run.

## Balance sheet
End-2025: net debt ₩377.3bn (down ₩61bn YoY), 부채비율 84.4%, 차입금의존도 29.5%. Operating cash flow is ~₩200bn/yr and the rating is A+ (stable). The ₩210bn Grand Hyatt Incheon West Tower purchase (agreed Sep 2025, by PSS) will add to debt if funded by borrowing (not verified). No CB/rights issues were found.

## Growth runway
Growth comes from adding 769 rooms at Paradise City (1,270 total) and from Chinese visa-free and Japanese demand. Organic growth is mid-to-high single digit, and it is a mature casino market with competition from GKL, Inspire (Mohegan) and Macau/Japan IRs.

## Key risks
China policy and geopolitics. VIP hold-rate volatility. Competition from Inspire IR next door. Rising labour costs. Losses while the acquired Hyatt is integrated. Minority leakage to Sega Sammy. Auditor unverified.
