## Business in two sentences
Seoho supplies the electrical drive, control and automation packages (including remote/automated STS and ARMG crane systems) that crane makers and terminal operators install on container cranes. Customers such as Busan New Port terminals (e.g. the Dongwon Global Terminal DTQC order) and overseas ports pay for these systems because the crane cannot run without them, and automation upgrades raise terminal productivity.
