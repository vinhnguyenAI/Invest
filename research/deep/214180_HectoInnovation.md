## Business in two sentences
Hecto Innovation sells payment and identity infrastructure to merchants and platforms: phone-based identity verification, bank-account "simple payment" and virtual accounts (through its subsidiary Hecto Financial). Merchants pay per transaction or verification because they need regulated KYC and cheaper-than-card payment rails. A fast-growing healthcare subsidiary also sells health-supplement brands direct to consumers.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩409.5bn (TTM 3Q25–2Q26)
- Normalised attributable net margin: **6.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised attributable NI: ₩26.6bn → **normalised EY 15.2%** (P/E 6.6x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. Attributable NPM was 7.9% (FY23), 8.7% (FY24) and ~7.5% est. (FY25). Consolidated OPM has been stable at 13–15%. 6.5% sits below every recent year to absorb minority leakage from Hecto Financial (Hecto Innovation owns only part of it) and the lower-margin healthcare mix.
  2. Drivers. Revenue grows with transaction volumes (e-commerce, virtual accounts for crypto exchanges and remittance) and with supplement sales. The 2025–26 OP squeeze comes from investment in stablecoin, global remittance and wallet infrastructure, which management says will roll off. 2Q26 NI −33% shows the leakage and investment cost.
- Bear case: 4.0% (healthcare marketing costs, PG fee compression, stablecoin losses) → ₩16.4bn → **EY 9.4%**, still above the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Founder 이택상 (의장) restructured his holdings in 2025, moving shares held via treasury and affiliates into his personal name. His stake is "close to 40%" (dealsite), described as responsible management and overhang removal. CEO 이현철 is a hired executive. The structure is a group parent of listed 헥토파이낸셜, with conglomerate-discount and related-party risks.

## Balance sheet
Not verified. The fintech subsidiary holds customer settlement funds, which inflate liabilities, so 부채비율 must be read ex-float. Check DART for borrowings, CBs and treasury shares.

## Growth runway
Steady ~12–25% quarterly growth. Drivers are the healthcare subsidiary (+40% in 2025), IT services (+17.5% in 2Q26), fintech (+11.2%) and the optional stablecoin and cross-border remittance business.

## Key risks
Minority leakage and holding discount. Regulation of PG and identity verification. Crypto-exchange dependence of virtual-account volume. The stablecoin pivot could absorb capital. Fad risk in health supplements. Owner not CEO.
