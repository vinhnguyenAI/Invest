## Business in two sentences
Krafton develops and publishes games, chiefly the PUBG: Battlegrounds franchise. It makes money from PC/console sales and in-game items, and from mobile PUBG (BGMI in India and a licence/royalty from Tencent's versions elsewhere). Players pay for cosmetics, passes and new titles. Newer IP (Subnautica 2, inZOI) is being added on top of the PUBG cash engine.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩4,452bn (TTM to 1H26, derived from verified FY25 and 1H26 figures)
- Normalised net margin: **28%**
- Normalised net income: ₩1,247bn → **normalised EY 14.2%** (P/E 7.1x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. Over FY21–25, OPM ran 32–44% and NPM 22–48%. FY25 was depressed by heavy IP and new-business investment, the Q4 one-off and earn-out accruals. FY24 was inflated by FX and financial income. A mid-point OPM of ~36%, plus interest income on net cash, less ~22% tax, gives ~28% NPM.
  2. Peer check: global live-service publishers with one dominant franchise (e.g. NetEase, Nexon) earn net margins in the 20–30% range.
- Bear case: 20% NPM on TTM revenue (PUBG fatigue, Subnautica 2 launch revenue not repeating, more M&A earn-outs) gives ₩890bn and **EY 10.1%**. A harsher bear, 22% on FY2025 revenue (no repeat of 2026 launch sales), gives ₩732bn and **EY 8.3%**, just under the hurdle.
