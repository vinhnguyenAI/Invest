## Business in two sentences
Daeyang makes lighting, switchboards and electrical systems for ships, which it sells to Korean shipyards (HD Hyundai, Samsung, Hanwha Ocean), and pressure sensors for car makers' tier-1s. Through a subsidiary it also makes batteries for navy submarines. Shipyards buy because Daeyang is the incumbent certified supplier with ~#1 domestic share, and each vessel requires a full lighting and electrical fit-out.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩239.8bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **7%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩16.8bn → **normalised EY 10.2%** (P/E 9.8x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. Margins swing with the shipbuilding cycle: OPM −7.6% (FY22 trough, steel and copper costs and low ship deliveries), 2.4% (FY23), 8% (FY24) and 11.7–12.3% (FY25/TTM, peak deliveries plus sensor ramp). A through-cycle average is roughly 5–8% OPM. Non-operating income (financial assets) has pushed NI above OP, which I don't fully capitalise.
  2. Drivers. Korean yards have a 3+ year backlog (LNG carriers, container ships), so deliveries stay high into 2028. Auto-sensor revenue grew from ₩23.6bn (2024) to ~₩37bn (2025E), with ₩50bn (2026E, LS) diversifying away from ships. Defence batteries ride the submarine programmes. At 7% NPM a lot of the upcycle is already removed.
- Bear case: 4% (shipbuilding normalises, copper/steel up, sensor pricing pressure) → ₩9.6bn → **EY 5.8%**, which fails the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Founder 서영우 founded the company in 1977 (as 대양전기제작소, Busan) and holds 59.34% (59.45% with related parties). It is a low-float, owner-dominated company. Don't confuse it with 대양금속 or 신대양제지, which are unrelated "대양" companies.

## Balance sheet
Not verified. Cash and equivalents were ₩39.3bn (FY23) and ₩34.9bn (FY24), against no stated figure for borrowings. Investment securities are likely material given the non-operating income. Check DART for debt, 부채비율 and any CB.

## Growth runway
The shipbuilding upcycle (deliveries are rising through 2027–28). Auto pressure sensors are growing 30–50%/yr off a small base. Defence (submarine batteries, naval lighting and electrics). 1Q26 was soft (−8.5%) but 2Q26 hit a record ₩70.8bn.

## Key risks
Cyclicality of shipbuilding. Copper and steel input costs. Customer concentration in Korean yards. A founder in his 70s+ with succession unclear. Illiquid stock. The balance sheet is unverified.
