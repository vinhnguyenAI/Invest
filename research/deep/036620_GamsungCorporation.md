## Business in two sentences
Gamsung holds the Snow Peak apparel licence (signed in 2019 and renewed in Feb-2025). It sells outdoor and "gorpcore" lifestyle clothing through ~186 department-store, outlet and agency stores plus online in Korea, and now China via a partner. Customers pay brand premiums for a Japanese premium-outdoor label, and Gamsung keeps a ~17% OPM after royalties.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩267.0bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **13%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩34.7bn → **normalised EY 10.9%** (P/E 9.2x)
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. NPM was 13.3% (FY24) and 14.1% (FY25). OPM was 13.8–18.1% over FY22–25, and ~0.78× OPM of 16.7% gives ~13%. That is in line with premium-licence apparel peers (F&F-type licence models earn 15–25% OPM at scale).
  2. Drivers. The scale benefits of a 186-store network are offset by China start-up costs and down-jacket weather risk. 13% assumes no royalty step-up and modest China losses.
- Bear case: 10% NPM (royalty hike at the next renewal, warm winter discounting, China losses) → ₩26.7bn → **EY 8.3%**.

## Owner & governance
Founder-CEO 김호선 holds 23.98% (29.46% with related parties) and has repeatedly bought shares in the open market. The company targets returning ≥50% of NI via dividends and buybacks with cancellation. The listed shell was formerly 버추얼텍 (Virtualtek), so check its legacy CB history on DART (memory).

## Balance sheet
Indirect only: liabilities/equity ~55% (investing.com) and seasonal working capital (pre-buying inventory, receivables collected ~2 months later per dealsite). Buybacks are funded from operating cash flow. Net cash/debt is unverified.

## Growth runway
China via a partner (target 20–30 stores in 2026; the first Guangzhou store took ₩220m on day 1). Exports of ₩35bn targeted for 2026. Han-yang forecasts FY26 revenue of ₩296bn (+18.5%) and OP of ₩55bn. The domestic store base is maturing, which shows in 2Q26's +9%.

## Key risks
Licence dependency on Snow Peak Japan (from memory: taken private in 2024, which raises renewal and royalty uncertainty). Fashion-cycle risk around gorpcore. Weather. China execution. A single brand.
