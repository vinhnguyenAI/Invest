## Business in two sentences
Poled designs and sells premium baby car seats, ventilated/heated seat liners and other childcare products under its own brand. Through consolidated subsidiaries IVG and Yupang it also sells baby appliances, to Korean parents through e-commerce and retail. Parents pay a premium for safety and comfort. Spend per child is rising even as births are low (Korean kids/baby goods market ₩6.6tn in 2020 → ₩7.7tn).

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩79.9bn (FY2025). TTM is likely higher but is not verified.
- Normalised net margin: **8.5%**
- Normalised net income: ₩6.8bn → **normalised EY 10.8%** (P/E 9.2x) vs hurdle 8.5%. On reported FY25 NI (₩8.4bn) the EY is 13.4%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. OPM has been 12–13% for two years. After the IPO the pre-IPO instrument and finance costs should fall away. A 13% OPM less ~20% tax gives ~10% NPM, and I haircut that to 8.5% for rising marketing and new-product spend and for lower-margin appliance distribution (IVG/Yupang).
  2. Peers in branded baby/consumer durables (e.g. Korean premium childcare brands) typically earn 5–10% net. The Airlove liner is seasonal, peaking in Q3, so one strong year can flatter the margin.
- Bear case: 5% NPM. Competition or copycats in seat liners, a fading fad, and lower-margin distribution revenue growing as a share of the mix. That gives ₩4.0bn and **EY 6.4%**, below the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
- 이형무, founder and CEO, holds 13.02% after the IPO (14.57% before). The largest holder group incl. pact members holds 28.15% (31.50% before the IPO).
- About 30% is under a joint voting agreement with a 5-year lock-up, so there is no near-term threat to control (IB Tomato).
- Lock-up release at day 100 (14 Aug 2026) raised the tradable float from 41% to 71%, an overhang.

## Balance sheet
- IPO: 2.6m new shares at ₩5,000 = ₩13.0bn, for capacity, R&D and global marketing. Net cash after the IPO is probably positive, but this is **not verified**.
- No going-concern issues are known. Pre-IPO financing instruments may have caused the FY24 NI gap; this needs checking.

## Growth runway
- Company guides to ₩120bn of revenue in FY2026 (1Q26 was 21% of that). Q3 is the peak season for the Airlove ventilated liners, and new products are planned for Q4.
- Possible overseas expansion with IPO funds.
- Part of the growth comes from consolidating IVG and Yupang. The organic split is unverified.

## Key risks
- Very short public track record (listed May 2026). The stock rose 4x on day one and has since fallen 50% below the IPO price.
- Fad and product-cycle risk in hero products; Korea's low birth rate.
- Distribution of third-party brands (Baby Brezza etc.) at lower margins.
- Lock-up overhang; owner's personal stake of only 13%.
