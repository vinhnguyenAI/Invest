## Business in two sentences
Myungin makes and sells prescription CNS drugs to psychiatric clinics and hospitals through its own sales force, plus OTC consumer brands (이가탄, 메이킨) through pharmacies. Doctors keep chronic patients on stable regimens, and consumers buy heavily advertised OTC brands.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩293.1bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **27%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩79.1bn → **normalised EY 13.4%** (P/E 7.5x) vs hurdle 8.5%. Ex-net-cash (~₩280bn+), the EY is ~25%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":** OPM has been ≥30% for 8 straight years (33–35% in 2020–24, 32.2% in FY25, 34.7% in 1H26), and NPM was 28% in FY25 and 31% in 1H26. 27% NPM assumes ~32% OPM with ~22% tax plus interest income. Korean drug-price cuts and OTC advertising are the main swing factors.
- Bear case: 20% (drug price cuts, generic competition in CNS) → ₩58.6bn → **EY 9.9%**, still above the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Founder 이행명 (회장) holds 50.88% and plans a gift that takes him to 43.62%. Family and related parties hold ~70–74%. The 2025 IPO was criticised as a succession/tax play and carries an overhang. The company paid its first interim dividend of ₩500/sh (₩7.3bn) in 2026, plus ₩21.9bn earlier.

## Balance sheet
Debt-free. Cash and short-term financial assets were ₩277.7bn at 1H25 (₩254.3bn at end-FY24). IPO primary proceeds come on top of this. No CB/BW.

## Growth runway
Mid-single-digit growth. CNS prescriptions are stable, and the OTC brands are mature. The company plans a new plant and overseas expansion, but nothing yet shows ≥10% growth.

## Key risks
Growth ceiling. Government drug-price cuts. OTC advertising dependence. Overhang from the IPO and succession. Cash hoarding (low ROE on excess cash).
