## Business in two sentences
Coway buys or makes water purifiers, air purifiers, bidets and mattresses and rents them to households for a monthly fee that bundles filter changes and servicing by its ~13,000-strong "Cody" service network (estimate). Customers pay for clean water and air without upfront cost. Overseas, Malaysia is the second home market and is now ~1/3 of sales, with growth also in Thailand and the US.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩5,301bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **12.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩663bn → **normalised EY 9.1%** (P/E 11.0x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. NPM was 11.9% (FY22), 11.9% (FY23), 13.1% (FY24) and 12.4% (FY25). It is very stable because rental revenue is recurring and largely contracted. 12.5% is roughly the 4-year average.
  2. Drivers. OPM is steady at ~18%. Overseas mix (Malaysia margins are similar or higher) is rising. Input costs (resin, components) are a small share of revenue, and the service-network cost is semi-fixed. Rising interest expense from higher borrowings is the main drag below the OP line.
- Bear case: 11.0% (domestic saturation, mattress/new-category marketing, Malaysia competition, higher interest) → ₩583bn → **EY 8.0%**.

## Owner & governance
Netmarble acquired 25.08% in 2019–20. Netmarble's founder 방준혁 is Coway's board chairman and a registered director (re-elected March 2026), and Netmarble has said it will add ~₩150bn of Coway shares (2026 broker report). Day-to-day management is by CEO 서장원, a Netmarble executive. Bang's look-through economic stake in Coway is only ~6–10%. Shareholder returns rose under the value-up plan (higher dividends and buybacks).

## Balance sheet
Total borrowings rose from ₩1.48tn (end-2024) to ₩1.97tn (2Q25), driven by overseas rental assets, higher dividends and working capital. 부채비율 was 94.3% (2Q25), close to the 100% threshold. Net debt/EBITDA is ~1x on my estimate. Credit rating AA- (KIS/NICE). The balance sheet is acceptable but not net cash.

## Growth runway
Malaysia/Thailand are expanding at 20%+. The US/Indonesia are earlier stage. New categories (BEREX massage chairs and mattresses) are being added domestically. A broker forecasts 2026 revenue of ~₩5.7tn (+14%). Growth accelerated from 3–9% (FY22–24) to ~14–15% (FY25–1H26). If that holds, the 3y CAGR will cross 10% at FY26.

## Key risks
Domestic market saturation. Malaysia regulatory or competitive risk (Cuckoo, local players). Rising leverage to fund dividends. Related-party risk with Netmarble, which used Coway dividends to support its own balance sheet. Service-staff labour issues.
