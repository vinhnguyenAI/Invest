## What insiders and analysts say (the "teams on the ground" layer)
- **"The industry sees the core Buldak sauce supply structure staying in place for now."** Changing the recipe risks a consumer backlash, and the cost saving isn't worth it (dt.co.kr, Jul-2025, quoting unnamed industry officials).
- **"S&D handles 100% of the sauce that forms Buldak's core taste"** (same dt.co.kr industry source).
- **Counter-view:** "the soup is a ramen's core, so for security it is normal for an affiliate to make it in-house" (Dealsite, Jul-2025, industry official). Seoul Economic reported that Samyang had outsourced *most* liquid and powder soup ingredients on an OEM basis and intends to bring them in-house.
- **A securities-industry view:** the G&F purchase may be about **overall ramen capacity rather than sauce insourcing**, and "it won't be easy to shake an established core supply chain" (Insight/Smarttoday roundup).
- **Broker (Jul-2026):** S&D's customer is "accelerating localisation centred on North America", so S&D's revenue growth is likely to continue (Insight, 2026-07-10; snippet only).

## The new subsidiary: what G&F seems to have become
- In Mar-2026 the Samyang group (holding company Samyang Round Square) was reported to be growing a sauce portfolio through **삼양스파이스 (Samyang Spice)** (newdaily, 2026-03-05). The article cites ₩41.7bn revenue / ₩3.2bn operating profit, **which are G&F's figures**, so Samyang Spice is very likely the renamed G&F. *Inference; not confirmed on DART.*
- Stated mission: liquid-sauce technology for **ready meals (HMR), sauce-type seasonings and fusion cooking sauces**, aimed at consumers. That is a brand-extension play, not an explicit plan to make the Buldak ramen soup base.
- Samyang's own sauce/seasoning sales: ₩40.6bn (2024) → ₩68.7bn (2025), with a 2026 target of +50% and 1H26 already above all of 2024. That is still only ~3–4% of group revenue (Hankyung, 2026-10-01; KIND 1Q26 filing).

## Read-across
1. **Dependence has risen:** Samyang was 66% of S&D revenue at the 2021 listing and an estimated 70–90% now.
2. **Samyang has kept its options open:** every Samyang statement is non-committal ("reviewing", "nothing to announce"). No "S&D remains our partner" quote from Samyang was found anywhere.
3. **The actual flow of business is still positive:** S&D 2Q26 revenue +31%, and a broker expects growth to continue with Samyang's North American push.
4. **Samyang Spice looks aimed at consumer sauces, not ramen soup base,** which modestly lowers the near-term substitution risk.
5. **Net effect on the odds:** unchanged (full breakup ~10% / partial ~35% over 3 years). The absence of any Samyang commitment keeps the partial-shift risk alive. The best early warning would be a Samyang Spice or Wonju job ad or capex for **"소스 베이스" / "시즈닝 원료"** (sauce-base or seasoning ingredients), or S&D growth lagging Samyang's overseas growth.
