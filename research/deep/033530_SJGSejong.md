## Business in two sentences
SJG Sejong makes exhaust systems (mufflers, catalytic converters, exhaust manifolds) and related parts, and supplies them to Hyundai/Kia plants in Korea, North America, Asia and Brazil. Carmakers pay a parts supplier with long-term model awards because it is the qualified, localised source for each platform.
