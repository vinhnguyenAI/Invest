## Business in two sentences
Sejin fabricates ship superstructures (deckhouses) and LPG/ammonia cargo tanks next to HD Hyundai's Ulsan yards and sells them to shipbuilders that outsource these blocks. The yards pay for this to free up dock capacity and get on-time block delivery.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩386.7bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **10%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩38.7bn → **normalised EY 7.8%** (P/E 12.8x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":** NPM was ~6% in FY24 and 15% in FY25, and the TTM figure includes non-operating gains (NI > OP). Before the upcycle, OPM was typically 6–10%. 10% NPM assumes healthy LPG/ammonia tank pricing in a strong but not peak shipbuilding market. A captive block supplier's pricing is ultimately set by the yard.
- Bear case: 7% (yard insources blocks, steel prices rise, tank orders slow) → ₩27.1bn → **EY 5.5%**.

Not verified for FY25. Historically leveraged (120–150% 부채비율). Strong FY25–26 cash generation has probably improved it.

## Growth runway
Limited. Revenue is capped by HD Hyundai's block-outsourcing volumes. Profit growth came from mix and price, not volume, and contract liabilities are falling.

## Key risks
