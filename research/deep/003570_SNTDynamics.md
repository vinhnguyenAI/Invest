## Business in two sentences
SNT Dynamics (ex-통일중공업) makes automatic transmissions and power packs for Korean tracked vehicles, including the 1,500hp transmission for the K2 tank from the 4th production batch and K9-related parts, and sells them to Hyundai Rotem, Hanwha Aerospace and export programmes (Poland, Romania, Türkiye direct exports). It also makes machine tools and auto parts. Customers pay for qualified, domestically produced defence drivetrain components.

- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History and peers. Excluding the ₩42.5bn FY24 provision reversal, OPM was ~10.5% (dealsite). FY25 OPM was 12.0% and TTM 10.2%, with 2Q26 down to 8.8%. Net margin is roughly OPM less tax plus interest income, i.e. about 9–10%. Defence-component peers earn 6–12% OPM.

- Bear case: 7% (export deliveries pause, mix shifts to civil machine tools) → ₩52.6bn → **EY 5.8%**.

## Owner & governance
Chairman 최평규 founded the SNT group (1979), acquired 통일중공업 in 2003 and controls it via SNT홀딩스 (50.76% personal). SNT Dynamics' representative director is 박재석 (hired). That makes this a group affiliate with a hired CEO, which fails criterion 4 as written, although 최평규 is known as a hands-on chairman. The company has a high dividend payout (₩1,800/share for FY25).

## Growth runway

## Key risks
