## Business in two sentences
Wontech makes energy-based aesthetic devices, chiefly the Oligio monopolar-RF skin-tightening system, plus HIFU and lasers. It sells the devices and recurring single-use tips to dermatology and aesthetic clinics in Korea and increasingly overseas (US, Asia), and the clinics pay because the procedures earn them high-margin non-insured fees.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩167.3bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: 22% (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩36.8bn → **normalised EY 9.0%** (P/E 11.1x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. Gross margin is ~67–71% (1Q26 71.1%) and OPM was ~33% in FY25. The NPM of 22.4% (FY25) and ~24% (TTM) is in line with Korean aesthetic-device peers (Classys ~35–40% NPM, Jeisys ~20%). 22% sits slightly below TTM to allow for heavier US marketing and CB fair-value noise.
  2. Mix is shifting toward consumables and exports, which supports the margin. Advertising (Oligio TV ads) and US expansion costs are the swing factor.
- Bear case: 18% (US price competition, ad spend stays high, CB valuation losses) → ₩30.1bn → EY 7.4%.

## Owner & governance
Founder-controlled (53.1% incl. related parties in 2023). The chairman made small open-market purchases (2024) and the company has a ₩6bn buyback trust. Repeated 0%-coupon private CBs are a negative capital-allocation signal: a highly profitable company is diluting minorities.

## Balance sheet
The May-2026 CB raised ₩50bn of operating money and ₩25bn of refinancing. Gross debt is now at least ₩75bn. Cash is unverified. CB conversion at the current price would add ~10% to the share count.

## Growth runway
Exports are growing fast (1H26 overseas revenue +21.5%), with the US the standout. The installed base drives recurring tip revenue. Growth slowed to +13.6% in 1H26 from +36% in FY25.

## Key risks
CB dilution. Competition in RF/HIFU from Classys, Jeisys and Lutronic. US regulatory and channel risk. Advertising-driven margin swings.
