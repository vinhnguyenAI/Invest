## Business in two sentences
Digital Daesung sells online lecture passes (대성마이맥) for the Korean college entrance exam (수능) to high-school students and repeat test-takers. It also runs residential boarding academies, including a 1,200-seat medical-school-prep campus, where parents pay high monthly fees for full-time study management.
