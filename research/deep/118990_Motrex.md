## Business in two sentences
Motrex designs and makes in-vehicle infotainment hardware (AVN head units, embedded dash-cams, displays) and sells it mainly to Hyundai/Kia and other OEMs for local-market and export cars. Through its listed subsidiary 전진건설로봇 (KOSPI 079900) it also sells concrete pump trucks to construction contractors, a large share of them overseas.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩701.7bn (TTM, roughly equal to FY2025)
- Normalised net margin: **3.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩24.6bn → **normalised EY 16.9%** (P/E 5.9x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. NPM has run 1.8–6.8% over FY21–TTM (FY21 attributable NI ~₩12bn on ₩437bn, about 2.7%). The FY22 6.8% came with peak OEM volumes and a weak KRW. FY25 1.8% was hit by a Q4 loss. 3.5% is about the middle of the range and equals TTM.
  2. Drivers. OPM is normally 6–10%, but heavy interest on acquisition debt (and the coming 두올 debt) plus the minority share of 전진건설로봇 take ~3–5pp off between OP and attributable NI. The infotainment business is a tier-1 supplier with thin margins and OEM price-downs, and the concrete-pump business is cyclical with construction.
- Bear case: 1.8% (the FY2025 level: OEM price cuts, a construction downturn, higher interest after 두올) → ₩12.6bn → **EY 8.7%**, which only just clears the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Founder-chairman 이형환 has 28.56% personally and 38.54% with related parties (aggregator, *unverified*). The group is building a holding-style structure (모트렉스에이디엠 → 모트렉스이에프엠 → 두올). That structure uses FI money (JKL) with drag-along and return-guarantee clauses that put the burden on Motrex, which adds complexity and creates a risk of value leaking to minority shareholders.

## Balance sheet
- 부채비율 157.1% at 1Q (year not stated; dealsite article on the 전진건설로봇 IPO). Proceeds from the IPO secondary sale (≤₩24.2bn) went to repaying acquisition finance, and the company itself said 부채비율 would only fall to ~145–147%.
- Free cash flow was −₩31.4bn in 2024 (IB토마토), with HQ construction capex rising to ₩43.5bn.
- 두올 acquisition (binding MOU Feb 2026, total ~₩145bn for 62.2%). On completion, consolidated debt and assets would roughly double. The deal was not confirmed closed as of June 2026.
- Net debt: not verified. It is likely well over ₩100bn (estimate).

## Growth runway
FY25 growth of +19.7% came from new global-OEM programs and pump-truck exports, but 1H26 was flat (1Q −5.4%, 2Q +5.5%). The planned 두올 deal (seat fabrics/interiors) would add inorganic revenue, not organic growth.

## Key risks
High leverage plus another leveraged M&A deal. Dependence on Hyundai/Kia. Tier-1 margin squeeze. A construction-cycle downturn hitting concrete pumps. Complex structure with FI put/guarantee terms. Auditor unverified.
