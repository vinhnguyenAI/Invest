## Business in two sentences
Dong-A Geological is a specialist civil-engineering contractor that bores tunnels with its own TBM/shield machines and does ground improvement, piling and foundation works for subways, GTX rail, power and utility tunnels, and overseas projects (Singapore, Hong Kong, etc.). Public agencies and general contractors pay it per project because few firms own the TBM fleet and know-how.

## Owner & governance
The company is PE-controlled. Crescendo bought the founder's shares in 2019, converted a CB to reach ~32.6% and holds 34.23% today. Founder 이정우's remaining stake is unverified. The sale has stalled on price (~₩300bn ask). A change of control is pending, and future management is unknown.
