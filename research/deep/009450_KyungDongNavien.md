## Business in two sentences
Navien makes gas boilers (the domestic leader, ahead of 귀뚜라미) and condensing tankless water heaters and combi-boilers. It sells them through plumbing distributors and wholesalers to installers and homeowners, mainly in North America, where it leads the premium condensing tankless segment, and in Korea. Customers pay for energy-efficient, compact units that replace tank heaters. The company is extending into heat pumps and HVAC.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩1,558.2bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **7.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩116.9bn → **normalised EY 12.2%** (P/E 8.2x) vs hurdle 8.5%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History. NPM was 4.6%, 6.9%, 9.2% and 6.0% from FY22 to FY25. FY25 would be ~8% without the one-off deferred-tax charge (4Q25 effective tax rate 65.6%). That gives a FY23–25 average of ~7.5–8% excluding one-offs. 1H26's 15.5% is inflated by tariff-rate relief and a 2Q26 tariff refund, so I exclude it.
  2. Drivers. North American condensing tankless units carry high margins, and their share of the mix keeps rising. Steel, copper and resin costs, freight and the KRW/USD rate swing margins by 2–4pp (in FY22, raw-material inflation cut OPM to 5.2%). US tariffs are the key external variable: 3Q25 OPM fell to 2.6% when a 25% tariff hit. 7.5% assumes tariffs in a "normal" negotiated range and a USD/KRW rate near 1,350–1,400.
- Bear case: **5%** (tariffs re-escalate, the US housing/renovation market slows, the KRW strengthens) → ₩77.9bn → **EY 8.1%**, just below the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Third-generation succession is under way. 손연호 (74) is chairman and CEO, and his son 손흥락 was promoted to vice-chairman and co-CEO in Mar-2025. The family controls the company through ㈜경동원 (56.7%). Shareholder returns are weak: the payout ratio is only ~7.6% (Digital Times). No CB/BW was found.

## Balance sheet
Leverage is rising but not alarming. 부채비율 crossed 90% in 2025, short-term borrowings are up sharply, and operating cash flow has been weak for two years because working capital and capex (US logistics, Korean plant expansion) absorbed cash. With a TTM EBITDA of roughly ₩250bn, net debt/EBITDA is probably still < 1.5x (estimate). Verify the FY2025 and 1H26 balance sheets on DART.

## Growth runway
North America (tankless conversion, combi-boilers, the push into heat pumps and HVAC) and smart-home products are credible long-run drivers. The domestic boiler market is mature. Growth slowed in 2Q26 (−1.0%) after tariff-driven pre-buying in 2025. FY26 revenue growth is likely mid-to-high single digit, which keeps the 3y CAGR below 10%.

## Key risks
US tariff policy (25% tariffs crushed 3Q25 margins). US housing and renovation cycles. The KRW/USD rate. Metal input costs. Execution of the HVAC and heat-pump expansion. Rising leverage and weak operating cash flow. A low payout ratio and a controlling holding company.
