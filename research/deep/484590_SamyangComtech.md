## Business in two sentences
Samyang Comtech makes ballistic ceramic armour modules for main battle tanks (the K2, including the Poland export programme) and armoured vehicles, plus ceramic body-armour plates and vests. Defence primes and governments pay for certified ballistic protection, and the company claims the world's largest ballistic-ceramic capacity.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩154.7bn (FY25; the TTM figure is lower because of 1H timing)
- Normalised net margin: 14% (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩21.7bn → **normalised EY 8.0%** (P/E 12.4x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin:** OPM rose from 1% to 8% to 13% to 17% (FY22–25) and then 21% in 1H26 as K2 export volumes filled the ceramic plant. 14% NPM matches FY25. Upside comes if the K2 Poland second contract and other exports sustain the 20%+ OPM, but defence-cost-plus pricing and lumpy acceptance argue against capitalising the peak.
- Bear case: 10% NPM (K2 programme gaps, body-armour price competition) → ₩15.5bn → **EY 5.7%**.

## Owner & governance
Control sits with two corporate shareholders and related parties (~56.8% total). 삼양화학공업 holds 19.77%. The listed CEO is a professional manager with a token stake. Lock-ups expired in Aug-2026, and the major holders stated they have no intention to sell.

## Balance sheet
Likely net cash after the IPO, with large customer prepayments (₩61.9bn). Not verified.

## Growth runway
K2 tank exports (Poland and others), global demand for body armour and the ceramic capacity expansion. The CEO targets +50% revenue within 5 years. Revenue is lumpy and depends on acceptance and inspection timing.

## Key risks
Dependence on a single programme (K2 / Hyundai Rotem). Lumpy revenue recognition. Defence budget and export-approval politics. Lock-up overhang.
