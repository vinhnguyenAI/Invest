## Business in two sentences
Seoyon E-Hwa designs and makes interior modules (door trims, seats, headliners, bumpers) and supplies them just-in-time to car assembly plants of Hyundai-Kia (Korea, US, India, Europe, etc.) and some global OEMs. The OEM pays per vehicle built on long-term model-cycle contracts, so revenue tracks the customer's production volumes and model mix.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩4,680bn (TTM estimate = FY2025 4,505 + 1H26 ~2,440 − 1H25 ~2,263)
- Normalised net margin: **2.5%**
- Normalised net income: ₩117bn → **normalised EY ~40%** (P/E ~2.5x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal"**:
  1. History: net margin ~2.1% (2022), ~4.6% (2023), ~3.7% (2024), 1.35% (2025) → 4-year average ~2.9%; 2.5% sits below the average.
  2. OPM has been stable at 3.6–5.4% through 2022–25 even as volumes swung; Q1'26 OPM recovered to ~4.1%. 2025 net income collapsed while OP rose 5%, i.e. the drop was below the operating line (non-operating losses/impairments/FX/tax — not identified, possibly US-tariff-related or overseas subsidiary impairments, as in 2022). Treating that as partly non-recurring supports ~2.5%.
  3. Peers (Korean tier-1 interior/module suppliers) typically earn 1.5–3% net through the cycle; Hyundai-Kia's US/India expansion gives volume and utilisation.
- What would make the margin lower (bear case): US tariffs squeezing OEM pricing pass-through, continued impairments of China/other overseas subsidiaries, rising interest cost on a leveraged balance sheet, minority-interest leakage. Bear 1.3% (= FY2025 level) → ₩61bn → **EY ~21%** — still far above hurdle.
