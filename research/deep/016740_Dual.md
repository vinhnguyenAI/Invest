## Business in two sentences
Dual makes automotive seat fabrics, finished seat covers and other interior parts (including airbag cushions) in Korea, China, the US, Europe and elsewhere. It sells to seat makers and OEMs, mainly Hyundai/Kia and global programmes. Volumes track the car models it is designed into.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩796.1bn (FY2025)
- Normalised net margin: **2.5%**
- Normalised net income: ₩19.9bn → **normalised EY 17.4%** (P/E 5.7x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. FY24 NPM was 2.2% and FY25 3.9%. FY25 was flattered by a strong Q3 (NI ₩12.4bn). OPM swung from 2.3% (FY22) to 6.1% (FY25), and 1H26 OPM is back at 3.5%. A mid-cycle OPM of ~4% less interest and tax gives a NPM of ~2.5%.
  2. Auto-interior peers in Korea earn 1–4% net. The business is a price-taker against OEM price-downs, with raw material (yarn/PET, leather) and labour-cost exposure.
- Bear case: 1.5% NPM (FY22-like utilisation, plus higher interest if the new owner levers the group). That gives ₩11.9bn and **EY 10.4%**.
- Cheap, but it fails on growth and on ownership.

## Owner & governance
- Previous controller: IHC and related parties (founder 조인회 family), ≈45.6–47.6%.
- Feb 2026 binding MOU, then an SPA on 30 Apr 2026. Motrex EFM buys 13.64m shares (₩111.8bn) from IHC and 4.2m shares (15.7%, ₩33.4bn) from 프리미어성장전략엠앤에이, for a total of ₩145.2bn, ~62.2% and ~₩8,195/share. Closing was set for 31 Jul 2026, and the largest-holder change to "모트렉스이에프엠 외 1인" was reported (dealsite, knowingasset).
- Motrex EFM is the former Hanmin/Jesung Naejang. Its 100% parent is SPC Mobilis, co-founded by Motrex and **JKL Partners**. Reports cite acquisition financing secured on Dual shares and assets, and drag-along/call-option terms with JKL (dealsite, Jun 2026).
- **Not owner-run in the criterion's sense.** The new controller is a leveraged, PE-backed holding chain, and the founder of Motrex (이그림) is not a Dual operator.

## Growth runway
- Limited. Revenue tracks Hyundai/Kia and global programme volumes. After the FY2023 seat-cover ramp-up, growth has run at about 3% a year.

## Key risks
- Leveraged change of control; possible cash extraction or related-party dealings with the Motrex group.
- OEM price-downs and input costs; thin margins.
- The ~₩8,195 control premium was paid to the old owners only. Minorities got no tender offer at that price (the price is now ₩3,990).
