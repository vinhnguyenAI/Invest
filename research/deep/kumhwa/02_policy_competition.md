# Kumhwa PSC (036190): policy, industry and competitors
*Research date 2026-10-16. Angle: outside sources only (government, press, sell-side on peers). The company's own statements are covered in a separate file. 18 web searches; WebFetch was not used, so all facts come from search-result snippets. Re-check key numbers against the originals.*

## 1. Coal phase-out timetable
- **11th Basic Plan (2024–2038), finalised Feb-2025.** Old coal units converting to LNG: **14.1 GW over 2025–2036**. The **12 units** reaching their 30-year design life in 2037–38 go to carbon-free sources (pumped hydro, hydrogen or ammonia co-firing); per one summary, 6 of the 12 go to hydrogen-only or CHP (3.4 GW). Working-draft generation mix: coal 19.7%→17.4% by 2030 and →10.3% by 2038; LNG 22.9%→25.1% by 2030, then 11.1% by 2038. Total thermal capacity is capped, so an LNG conversion must not add net thermal MW. Sources: https://shinkim.com/kor/media/newsletter/2761 ; https://www.shinkim.com/kor/media/newsletter/2478 ; https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2024/05/31/2024053100068.amp.html (2024-05-31)
- **Press tally:** about **28 coal units convert to LNG by 2035**, starting with Taean #1, and most end operation by 2036. 서부발전 closes 8 of its 11 coal units by 2037. Sources: https://v.daum.net/v/20251127110228123 (2025-11-27) ; https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609021013275526 (2026-09)
- **Lee Jae-myung government:** "2040 탈석탄" is a campaign pledge and a national policy task. Korea **joined the PPCA (Powering Past Coal Alliance) at COP30 in Nov-2025**; Minister 김성환 (기후에너지환경부) announced it. Korea had 39.1 GW of coal capacity (2023) and coal supplied about 30% of generation in 2024. Sources: https://inews24.com/view/wrlted/1908405 ; https://www.heraldk.com/article/2025111817470943753 (2025-11-18) ; https://www.mt.co.kr/mtreport/4400
- **12th Basic Plan, draft only, not finalised as of mid-Oct 2026.** At the 7th public forum on 2026-09-18, the government presented a "2040 coal exit" proposal:
  - **60 coal units are operating now.** On a 30-year life, **39 retire by 2038**. The other **21 units (19.1 GW)** still have life left after 2040; the base case retires them early over 2037–39.
  - **Up to 10 of the 21** would be kept as idle "안보전원" (emergency reserve that runs only in emergencies).
  - Replacement: LNG and pumped hydro. Examples: 영흥 1·2 replaced by LNG in the capital region, 당진 5·6 relocated as LNG CHP in Honam for the chip cluster, some private coal moved to LNG CHP after 2030. Also BESS-tender preference and possible SMR conversion.
  - The presenter said it is "not the final plan." The minister targets a government draft around October, then National Assembly reporting. The 2040 demand forecast was raised by about 190 TWh in four months (AI and chips), and NGOs warn the 안보전원 exemption could hollow out the 2040 goal.
  - Sources: https://www.etnews.com/20260918000144 (2026-09-18) ; https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026091810200022912 (2026-09-18) ; https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026091845741 (2026-09-18) ; https://forourclimate.org/ko/newsroom/1250
- **Nuclear is back:** two new large reactors stay in the plan, for completion in 2037–38. Source: https://www.mt.co.kr/economy/2026/01/27/2026012620022995944 (2026-01-27)
- **Genco restructuring:** on 2026-09-04 the government announced it will merge the 5 thermal gencos into one company, "㈜한국발전" (100% owned by KEPCO), with a target launch of 2027-10-01 under a special law. Headquarters staff fall from about 2,400 to 1,800. No source says 한전KPS will be folded in. Experts flag concentration of buyer power. Sources: https://newsseoul.co.kr/news/view/1065595224060757 (2026-09) ; https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2594931 (2026-06) ; https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026100323452527565 (2026-10-03)

## 2. What happens to maintenance demand
- **LNG combined-cycle needs less maintenance labour than coal.** Coal plants have pulverisers, boilers, coal and ash handling and FGD equipment; a gas plant "needs only gas pipes," so fewer staff. Unions say contractor and subsidiary workers, unlike genco employees, cannot practically be redeployed. Sources: https://v.daum.net/v/20251127110228123 (2025-11-27) ; https://www.pressian.com/pages/articles/2026052011322186948 (2026-05-20)
- **Gas-turbine maintenance is OEM- and technology-heavy.** This is exactly the slice now being reserved for 한전KPS (next bullets). Conversion and demolition works (partial demolition, HRSG replacement) give some one-off work. Example: SNT에너지 won a ₩128bn HRSG order for the Hadong 2·3 LNG replacement. Source: https://www.inews24.com/view/1820933
- **Taean accident and the in-sourcing fight (the key new risk):**
  - **Accident:** 2025-06, 김충현, a second-tier subcontract worker under 한전KPS, died at 태안화력. 서부발전's Taean 경상정비 contractors were **한전KPS, 금화PSC and 두산에너빌리티**, and **only 한전KPS used second-tier subcontractors**. Source: https://v.daum.net/v/20250619195314183 (2025-06-19)
  - **Court ruling:** 2025-08, the Seoul Central District Court found illegal dispatch for 24 한전KPS sub-subcontract workers. Source: https://www.newstomato.com/ReadNews.aspx?no=1273037
  - **First labour-government agreement:** 2026-02-10. 한전KPS will **directly hire about 600 of its own subcontract maintenance workers**. This set off a union-vs-union fight (한국노총 전력연맹 walked out) and a projection that labour cost rises 15%. Sources: https://www.hankyung.com/amp/202602106733i ; https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2026/02/12/2026021200167.html (2026-02-12) ; https://www.hankyung.com/article/202602178527i (2026-02-17)
  - **Final agreement:** 2026-07-14, the PM-office "발전산업 정의로운 전환 협의체" signed it after 21 meetings over 11 months. It confirms the 한전KPS direct-hire, promises state job-retraining support for the energy transition, and sets two tracks: energy transition and a "sustainable maintenance-industry structure." Source: https://www.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202607140093 (2026-07-14)
  - **Clause that hits private contractors:** per a 2026-07-24 report, the agreement text says **"maintenance of new high-performance combined-cycle plants, including gas turbines of 200 MW and above, will be performed by the maintenance public enterprise (한전KPS)."** Private firms call it a "gift of work" to 한전KPS in return for the direct-hire burden. The report says private work "will fall sharply if implemented" and that the clause shakes the competitive structure in place since 2003. Source: https://v.daum.net/v/20260724004612126 (2026-07-24)
- **What I could not confirm:**
  - Whether gencos must in-source *private* contractors' (e.g. Kumhwa's) staff. Every source so far concerns only 한전KPS's own subcontractors.
  - Whether existing 경상정비 contracts are re-tendered when a unit converts. A new combined-cycle unit is a new facility, so under the July clause its maintenance would go to 한전KPS by default.
  - Any separate 정의로운 전환 special act for private maintenance workers. The support found is retraining.

## 3. Competitors and market share
- **Shares:** 한전KPS has **about 50%** of power-plant maintenance, with **8 main private firms** competing (2026-07). Another 2026 research note puts it at **한전KPS about 44%, private about 56%**. Sources: https://v.daum.net/v/20260724004612126 ; https://stock.pstatic.net/stock-research/company/63/20260513_company_15531000.pdf (2026-05-13)
- **Private peers:** 금화PSC (largest private 경상정비 firm per eBest 2019), 일진파워, 수산인더스트리, 한국플랜트서비스, 옵티멀에너지서비스, 원프랜트. Their combined revenue grew from ₩253.9bn (2011) to ₩637.4bn (2018), a 14.1% CAGR with no down year. Source: https://file.alphasquare.co.kr/media/pdfs/company-report/이베스트20191007금화피에스시.pdf (2019-10-07)
- **How peers are diversifying (mostly into nuclear):**
  - **한전KPS:** 1Q26 revenue ₩352.4bn (+22%), driven by nuclear and pumped hydro at ₩149.9bn (+45%). Thermal planned-overhaul target cut from 101 to 97 units; nuclear overhauls rise from 13 to 20 in 2026. Romania Cernavoda O&M starts Sep-2027, and Czech work is a long-term hope. The **thermal mix is shrinking even at the incumbent**. Sources: https://stock.pstatic.net/stock-research/company/63/20260513_company_15531000.pdf ; https://stock.pstatic.net/stock-research/company/15/20260512_company_840415000.pdf (2026-05)
  - **수산인더스트리:** holds the most private nuclear maintenance qualifications. 1H26 revenue ₩178.3bn (+7.8%), OP ₩19.5bn (−6.3%), driven by domestic and Barakah (UAE) overhauls. Won a ₩93.6bn KHNP maintenance contract (2025-07, through 2027-06). Also pursuing i-SMR (subsidiary 수산이앤에스), defence electronics and wind O&M (오미산 wind farm). Sources: https://www.finance-scope.com/article/view/scp202608140026 (2026-08-14) ; https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/84649 (2025-07)
  - **일진파워:** Abu Dhabi subsidiary (2024) for Barakah maintenance; ₩27bn Cernavoda tritium-removal equipment deal with Doosan (2025-08); positioned as an AI-power and energy-infrastructure play. Sources: https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025082756941 (2025-08-27) ; https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026061608123404129 (2026-06-16)
  - **Kumhwa:** has the least nuclear exposure of the listed private peers. Its diversification is water treatment (금화정수), which is outside the power policy risk.

## 4. Growth areas in O&M
- **New LNG / combined-cycle:** 14.1 GW of coal-to-LNG under the 11th plan, plus 12th-plan relocations (영흥, 당진). The LNG fleet is growing, but **the July-2026 clause routes new ≥200 MW gas-turbine combined-cycle maintenance to 한전KPS**, so the biggest replacement pool may be largely closed to private firms. Older existing combined-cycle units and smaller or CHP units (private IPP and 집단에너지) look still contestable. *Inference.*
- **Nuclear:** two new APR units (2037–38), more overhauls, Barakah, Cernavoda and Czechia. Entry requires KHNP qualifications, held by 한전KPS, 수산 and 일진.
- **Offshore wind O&M:** gencos are building O&M ecosystems; for example, 중부발전 signed an MOU with 유니슨, and 서부발전 is retraining coal staff for offshore wind. The market is small and early. Source: https://www.theguru.co.kr/news/article.html?no=104246
- **Hydrogen and ammonia co-firing:** replacement for the 2037–38 units (e.g. 서인천 hydrogen or CHP by 2034-12). Too far out and too small to count.

## 5. Conclusion: threat level to Kumhwa, **MEDIUM-HIGH, rising**
1. Coal exit is now a firm political commitment (PPCA plus the 2040 pledge). 39 of 60 units retire by 2038 and the other 21 by 2037–40, with up to 10 idle as reserve; idle units need far less 경상정비. Kumhwa's coal base erodes from about 2028 and steeply in the 2030s.
2. LNG conversion does not replace coal one-for-one: combined-cycle maintenance needs much less labour, and gas-turbine work is technical and OEM-heavy.
3. **The bigger new threat is political, not technical.** The 2026-07-14 labour-government deal reserves maintenance of new ≥200 MW combined-cycle plants for 한전KPS. That takes much of the replacement LNG pool away from private firms like Kumhwa.
4. Partial offsets: Kumhwa did not use sub-subcontractors at Taean, so the direct-hire mandate targets 한전KPS, not it. Private IPPs and CHP plants stay open, and conversion and demolition give one-off work. Water O&M (금화정수) gives a non-power leg.
5. Peers (한전KPS, 수산, 일진) are pivoting to nuclear and overseas work, where Kumhwa has little presence. Treat the coal-related earnings stream as a decaying asset with a 10–15-year runway, and watch (a) how the July clause is implemented, (b) the final 12th Plan and 안보전원 count, and (c) the 2027 merged ㈜한국발전 procurement policy.
