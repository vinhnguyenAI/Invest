## 3. Coal-tied revenue today (ESTIMATE)

The inputs are partly sourced and partly assumed:
- Parent = 75.6% of group, i.e. ₩320.3bn (FY25, sourced).
- Nuclear = 21.4% of parent in 2023 (sourced). For FY25 I assume **~22%**: it is rising, but the ₩58.9bn Shin-Hanul install (~₩10bn/yr) began only in 2H25.
- Other non-power plant work (petrochem, LNG terminal, steel): assumed ~3% of parent.
- Thermal = 100 − 22 − 3 = **~75% of parent**, i.e. ₩240bn.
- Coal share of thermal: **assumed 80%** (range 70–90%). Private 경상정비 in Korea is overwhelmingly on the gencos' coal fleet, because boilers, coal handling, FGD and ash systems carry far more labour hours per MW than gas CCGTs. The company's flagship sites (Taean, Yeongheung) are coal.

| Case | Thermal % of parent | Coal % of thermal | Coal revenue (₩bn) | % of group revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 70% | 70% | 320.3 × 0.70 × 0.70 = 157 | **37%** |
| **Base** | 75% | 80% | 320.3 × 0.75 × 0.80 = **192** | **45%** |
| High | 80% | 90% | 320.3 × 0.80 × 0.90 = 231 | **55%** |

On profit the exposure is higher, at roughly 50–60% of group OP, because power O&M is ~88% of OP (estimate). 금화정수 may add some indirect coal exposure if it services thermal-plant water systems. That is unverified and not included.

## 4. Revenue at risk from coal retirements (ESTIMATE)

Assumptions:
- (a) The company's coal units retire in line with the national fleet. I could not verify that its sites are older or younger than average.
- (b) When a coal unit converts to LNG at the same site, the incumbent keeps about **40%** of the former maintenance revenue (lower O&M intensity for a CCGT). Outright closure keeps 0%. The net factor used is 1 − 0.4 = 0.6 of gross.
- (c) FY25 revenue is held flat at ₩423.5bn, so this shows the gap to fill, not a forecast.

Fleet loss (share of coal units/capacity gone):
- **Plan:** 2030 ≈ 20% (10th/11th Plan capacity path, ~39 GW → ~31–32 GW); 2036 ≈ 36% (14.1 GW LNG conversion out of ~39 GW).
- **Accelerated:** a straight line from 2026 to zero in 2040, giving 2030 ≈ 4/14 ≈ 30–35% and 2036 ≈ 10/14 ≈ 71%.

| Horizon / scenario | Gross coal revenue lost | % group (gross) | Net after LNG retention (×0.6) | % group (net) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2030 plan (20%) | 192 × 0.20 = ₩38bn | 9% | ₩23bn | **~5%** |
| 2030 accelerated (35%) | 192 × 0.35 = ₩67bn | 16% | ₩40bn | **~9.5%** |
| 2036 plan (36%) | 192 × 0.36 = ₩69bn | 16% | ₩41bn | **~10%** |
| 2036 accelerated (71%) | 192 × 0.71 = ₩136bn | 32% | ₩82bn | **~19%** |
| 2040 full exit (100%) | ₩192bn | 45% | ₩115bn | **~27%** |

Annualised drag, base plan: ₩41bn over 10 years ≈ ₩4bn/yr, about **1% of revenue per year**. Group growth of 12% organic (FY23→25) more than offsets that today. In the accelerated case the drag is ~₩8bn/yr, about 2%/yr, and it bunches after 2030.

## 6. Conclusion
1. Power-plant O&M (parent) is **~76% of revenue and ~88% of OP**. 금화정수 and other businesses are ~24% of revenue at roughly 5% OPM (derived, FY25).
2. Nuclear has risen to **~21% of parent revenue** (2023, KIRS), which leaves thermal at roughly 75%. The company does not disclose coal and LNG separately.
3. **Estimate:** about **45% of group revenue (range 37–55%)**, roughly ₩190bn, is tied to coal units today, and ~50–60% of OP.
4. **Estimate:** net revenue at risk is **~5% by 2030 and ~10% by 2036** under the 11th Plan, rising to **~9.5% / ~19%** if the 2040 full-exit target is followed linearly. Gross exposure before LNG re-capture is roughly 1.7× those figures.
5. The risk is gradual (about 1–2% of revenue per year) and is currently outweighed by nuclear and water growth. The biggest open item is the unit-level customer and site list in the DART 사업보고서, which would confirm or replace the 80% coal-of-thermal assumption.
