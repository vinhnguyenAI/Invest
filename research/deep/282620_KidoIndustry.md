## Business in two sentences
Kido Industry (founded 1980) cuts and sews high-spec outdoor jackets and motorcycle protective clothing in South/Southeast-Asian factories for Western brand owners. Brands pay per garment on seasonal orders, choosing Kido for technical fabrics/seam-sealing know-how and reliable delivery.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩346.6bn (FY2025; core apparel revenue — 기도스포츠 appears to have been equity-accounted, so its ₩126.7bn revenue is not in this figure — *inference*)
- Normalised net margin: **5.5%** (core, ex-기도스포츠)
- Normalised net income: ₩19.1bn → **normalised EY ~31%** (P/E ~3.2x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal"**:
  1. Core OPM has held at 9.3–9.5% for 2023–25 (GPM ~20%). Removing the ~40% of NI that came from 기도스포츠 leaves core NI ≈ ₩21bn ≈ 6.1% of 2025 revenue, matching the IPO's "LTM adjusted attributable NI ₩21.2bn".
  2. Build-up: 8.5% OPM − ~1.2% interest on ~₩85bn debt → ~7.3% pre-tax × ~0.78 = ~5.7%. Listed apparel-OEM peers (Youngone, Hansae, Hojeon) earn ~4–8% net through the cycle. 5.5% assumes no further 1Q26-type disruption but a modest margin give-back.
- What would make the margin lower (bear case): permanent loss/reduction of a key brand (Anta-owned brand's vendor consolidation), wage inflation in Bangladesh/Vietnam, USD/KRW swings on FX debt, start-up costs of the Bangladesh expansion. Bear 3.0% → ₩10.4bn → **EY ~17%**; a 20% revenue drop at 3% → ₩8.3bn → EY ~14% — still above hurdle.

## Owner & governance
- 박장희 (CEO, founder family) held 83.03% pre-IPO (alphasquare; another report 94.81% incl. treasury shares). IPO: 1.7m shares, 20.59% secondary (owner sell-down). Post-IPO stake estimated ~58% (5.86m shares outstanding) — *estimate*.
- **Red flag:** In Nov-2025 Kido split off the investment division holding 48.7% of 기도스포츠 (motorcycle helmets, 2025 revenue ₩126.7bn, ~40% of Kido's NI) and merged it into 기도스포츠, which "became entirely the owner's personal company" (Asia Economy). Value that belonged to the pre-IPO company left the listed perimeter — a minority-shareholder fairness issue.
- IPO priced at the top of the band (₩28,400) on LTM earnings despite a 1Q26 loss; shares fell 35% on day 1 and ~63% since.

## Balance sheet

## Growth runway
Management targets ₩500bn revenue by 2030 and +50% capacity post-listing. Runway credible only if the Anta-owned buyer's order gap is temporary (management says orders recovered from Q2) — unverified.

## Key risks

- Governance: related-party transfer of 기도스포츠 to owner; IPO overpricing.
- FX debt; labour-cost and political risk in Bangladesh/Myanmar.
