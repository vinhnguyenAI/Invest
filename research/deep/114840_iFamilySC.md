## Business in two sentences
iFamilySC owns rom&nd, a K-beauty colour-cosmetics brand whose lip tints and palettes are made by ODMs (Cosmax/Kolmar type). It sells them through retailers and distributors such as Japanese drugstores and variety shops, Olive Young, Qoo10 and Amazon to young women who pay for trend-led shades at mass-market prices. About 70% of sales are overseas, with Japan the largest market.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ₩225.6bn (TTM)
- Normalised net margin: **8.5%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩19.2bn → **normalised EY 14.2%** (P/E 7.0x) vs hurdle 8.5%. On an ex-net-cash basis (~₩52bn, estimate) the EY is ~23%.
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. History and peers. NPM was 14.0% in FY24 (peak), 9.8% in FY25 and 8.5% TTM. Asset-light K-beauty brands typically earn 8–15% OPM (Clio ~5–8% NPM, Amorepacific-like brands lower). 8.5% equals TTM and is well below the peak.
  2. Drivers. SG&A rose from ₩39.6bn (FY24) to ₩61.2bn (FY25) to fund overseas expansion (US, Europe, new SKUs), and quarterly OPM fell from 20.5% (1Q24) to 13.1% (1Q25) to 6.7% (1Q26). Inventory was normalised (₩73.7bn to ₩48.0bn). If marketing spend falls back toward ~25% of sales, a 10–12% OPM (~8.5% NPM after tax) is achievable. If it doesn't, TTM is already the floor.
- Bear case: 5.0% (marketing stays elevated, competition from indie K-beauty brands and pressure on Japanese channels) → ₩11.3bn → **EY 8.4%**, just below the hurdle.

## Owner & governance
Founder 김태욱 (ex-musician turned entrepreneur, CEO) holds ~27.6%, with co-CEO 김성현 alongside. The company pays out ~32% of NI as dividends. No CB/BW issuance was found.

## Balance sheet
Net cash is estimated at ₩52bn for FY25E (Hanwha 2025-05 projection) and was ₩33.9bn in FY23E (Meritz). 부채비율 is ~35–49% (broker estimates). The US subsidiary is in full capital impairment (dealsite). All of these figures are estimates and are not verified against the FY2025 BS.

## Growth runway
Growth has stalled. 1H26 grew +1.2% even though 2Q26 was a record quarter (₩58.7bn). The bull case depends on payback from US, Europe and China channel investments in 2H26. The DB증권 analyst cut the target price to ₩12,000, citing low visibility on profitability.

## Key risks
Single-brand fashion risk. Japan concentration and the JPY exchange rate. Marketing spend without a matching sales response. Low barriers to entry from ODMs. Losses at the US subsidiary.
