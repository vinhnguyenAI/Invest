## Business in two sentences
Yooshin is Korea's oldest listed civil-engineering consultancy (founded 1966, KOSDAQ 2002). It sells feasibility studies, design and construction supervision for roads, rail, airports, bridges and water projects, mostly to the Korean government and public agencies (MOLIT, KR, KEC, LH) plus some ODA/overseas work, and is paid fixed fees per contract. Since 2023 it has also run a small EPC business (incineration and power-plant packages), expanded through the Feb-2025 merger of 씨이테크.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: **₩429bn (TTM to Jun-2026)**. The FY2025 base of ₩385.5bn gives an EY of 13.4%.
- Normalised net margin: **2.5%**
- Normalised net income: ₩10.7bn → **normalised EY 15.0%** (P/E 6.7x) vs hurdle 8.5%
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. The 2022–24 margins of 6–7% were inflated by one-offs. Apartment-sale profit from 'e편한세상 시티 광교' (₩26bn revenue in 2021, ₩59bn in 2022, finished 2023) and a ~₩17.8bn asset-sale gain in 2024 explain most of the gap. Without them the core consultancy earns roughly 2–3% net, consistent with the 10-year average of about 2.5% and with listed peers (도화엔지니어링, 한국종합기술: 1–4% net).
  2. FY2025 net margin was 2.7%, despite EPC start-up costs and merger integration. 1H26 OP more than doubled on +25% revenue (operating leverage on a fixed engineer headcount). Engineering fees are labour cost-plus with little commodity or FX exposure.
  3. Demand driver: Korean SOC budgets and GTX/rail and airport (가덕도) pipelines support utilisation. The fee floor is set by the government engineering price standard (엔지니어링 대가 기준).
- Bear case 1.5% (a 2018–19 style loss year, or EPC cost overruns) → ₩6.4bn → **EY 9.0%**, still above the hurdle. At 1.0% the EY is 6.0%.

## Owner & governance
- Owner group 50.57% (Dec-2025, up from 50.44% in Dec-2021). Top holder 전경수 37.2%, 전경 9.07%, three others under 3% each (Simply Wall St / S&P data).
- 전경수 is listed among the 대표자 together with 박석성 and 장학성 (jasoseol). Earlier filings show professional co-CEOs (한영수, 박석성, from 2020). So family control is clear, and the owner appears to sit in the CEO line-up alongside hired engineer-CEOs. His exact title and whether he runs the business day to day are not verified.
- Free float is small (~49%) and daily turnover is about ₩156m. Liquidity is poor.

## Balance sheet
- Simply Wall St (latest report, likely Sep/Dec-2025): total assets ₩293.5bn, liabilities ₩145.8bn, equity ₩147.7bn (부채비율 ~99%). Debt ₩31.0bn (Koala Gains: ₩39.2bn, probably including leases), cash and short-term investments ₩57.8bn → **net cash ₩19–27bn**.
- Debt/equity rose from 13% to 21% over 5 years. In 2024 it paid ₩59bn for 60% of its Gangnam HQ building, and cash still exceeded borrowings (dealsite). PBR ≈0.35–0.5x.
- Watch-out: operating cash flow was very weak in the latest TTM (₩0.16bn), as EPC working capital built up and receivables piled up. No CB/BW or rights issue was found. Dividend about ₩2.7bn in 2025.

## Growth runway
- Revenue: 166.6 (2019) → 212.3 → 262.2 → 304.1 → 341.1 → 339.6 → 385.5 (2025) → TTM 429. The 2024 dip came from the end of the apartment project.
- 1H26 +25% YoY: EPC (₩41bn in 2025 and growing) plus the 씨이테크 merger plus SOC design demand. The 3y CAGR from FY2022 to TTM is about 10%.

## Key risks
- Thin core margin: a few loss-making contracts or EPC cost overruns can wipe out profit (2018–19 were loss years).
- Dependence on government SOC budgets; fee pressure.
- EPC diversification raises working capital and risk (weak OCF).
- Illiquid stock; family has no need to return cash. Auditor unverified.
