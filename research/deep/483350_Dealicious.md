## Business in two sentences
Dealicious runs Shinsang Market, the dominant (~90%+ share) B2B app where Dongdaemun clothing wholesalers list new items daily and retailers (online shops, boutiques) order. It also offers logistics, sourcing and global direct shipping. Wholesalers pay for ads and memberships to reach buyers, and buyers subscribe for services; no transaction fee is charged.

## Normalised earnings
- Revenue base used: ~₩33.7bn (TTM, estimate)
- Normalised net margin: **15%** (estimate)
- Normalised net income: ₩5.1bn → **normalised EY 9.6%** (P/E 10.4x)
- **Why this margin is "normal":**
  1. Mechanism. Ad and subscription revenue on an established platform has near-zero marginal cost. Opex fell from ₩30.9bn to ₩29.1bn while revenue rose 22%, so incremental margins are very high. 2Q26 OPM was 26%, and the company targets FY26 OP of ₩9bn on ₩37.2bn (24%).
  2. Normal NPM. An OPM of ~18% (the 1H26 level) plus interest on IPO cash, less ~20% tax once losses are used, gives ~15%. There is no multi-year history, so this is the key uncertainty.
- Bear case: 7% NPM (marketing and new SaaS such as 장끼노트 or global shipping reinvestment consume the operating leverage; Dongdaemun volumes shrink) → ₩2.4bn → **EY 4.5%**.

## Owner & governance
Founder co-CEOs plus a founder CFO. The post-IPO founder stake is unverified (~29.6% per Stage-1). Heavy VC overhang: only 5 of 441 institutions accepted lock-ups, and pre-IPO shares outnumber new shares (IB토마토). The stock is ~66% below its ₩7,000 IPO price.

## Balance sheet
Pre-IPO, the company was in full capital impairment because RCPS were booked as liabilities. All RCPS converted in Feb-2026. IPO proceeds (lead manager Korea Investment) should leave net cash. Size and 부채비율 are unverified.

## Growth runway
Digitising Dongdaemun wholesale: ads, memberships, B2B SaaS (장끼노트 AI bookkeeping) and global direct shipping to 95 countries. The ceiling is the size of Dongdaemun's wholesale market itself, which faces Chinese competition.

## Key risks
An unproven margin. Single-platform and single-market dependency. VC selling overhang. Dongdaemun's secular decline. Short listed history.
