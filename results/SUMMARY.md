## How to check the auditor on DART (2 minutes per company)
1. Go to <https://dart.fss.or.kr> and search the company name (Korean).
2. Open the **2025 사업보고서** (annual report, filed ~March 2026). Then either:
   - go to section **"감사인의 감사의견 등"** / **"외부감사에 관한 사항"**, or
   - open the attached **감사보고서** (audit report) and read the firm name on the last page.
3. **삼일회계법인 = PwC → exclude.** 삼정 = KPMG, 안진 = Deloitte, 한영 = EY; others are local firms.
4. Also check 1H26 **반기보고서** (half-year report) → 요약재무정보 (summary financials) for 부채비율 and cash vs. borrowings.

## Method and caveats
- Stage 1 triage combined model knowledge with a limited web-search budget. Some good companies may have been wrongly failed, especially obscure small caps marked "low" confidence in `triage_all.csv`.
- Stage 2 figures come from Korean financial aggregators, broker PDFs and news, not directly from DART (DART, KRX and Naver are blocked from the research environment). Each report marks *verified (source)* vs *estimate*.
- Normalised EY = revenue base × normalised **net** margin ÷ market cap on 2026-10-14. Net cash is not added back.
- Not investment advice.
